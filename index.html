// about

From building systems to reading the data inside them

I started my career writing the software that runs businesses — billing systems, inventory, point-of-sale, HR payroll — spending 4+ years elbow-deep in MySQL schemas and SQL queries that real companies depended on every day. That work taught me what data actually looks like before anyone cleans it: inconsistent, duplicated, and full of edge cases nobody documented.

That's exactly the background I now bring to data analysis and data science. Over the past year I've been applying that database instinct to full analytics workflows — EDA, feature engineering, and building machine learning models rigorously evaluated with confusion matrix, precision, recall, F1-score, and ROC-AUC — plus translating findings into dashboards non-technical stakeholders actually use, in Power BI, Excel, and Tableau.

I've also taught computer literacy, which sharpened my ability to translate technical concepts clearly for non-technical audiences. Currently based in Dhaka, Bangladesh, preparing for graduate study abroad while continuing to build out my data science portfolio.
